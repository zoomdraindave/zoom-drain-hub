means the date the Membership Agreement is executed by both parties.

means fats, oils, and grease.
1.6 “Member”
means the business entity identified in the Membership Agreement, and its authorized representatives.

means the credit card or ACH bank account on file with the Company as authorized by Member for recurring billing.

means the Company's standard non-member price for a given service at the time that service is performed, as published by the Company from time to time.
1.14 “Service Visit”
means a scheduled visit to Member's premises to perform grease interceptor or other plumbing services as specified in the Membership Plan.

Section 3

Membership Fees and Payment

3.1 Monthly Billing in Advance. The Membership Fee is charged to the Member Payment Method on the Payment Date of each month, in advance of services scheduled for that month. Member expressly authorizes Company to charge the Member Payment Method for the Membership Fee and any other amounts due under this Agreement.

3.2 Authorized Payment Methods. Member authorizes Company to charge either a credit or debit card on file, or an ACH bank account on file, as designated at enrollment. Member agrees to maintain a valid payment method on file at all times and to update payment information promptly upon any change.

3.3 Failed Payments. If a Membership Fee payment fails:

  • Company will notify Member and attempt to process the charge up to three (3) additional times over ten (10) business days.
  • Service will be suspended from the date of the failed payment and will not resume until payment is successfully collected.
  • If payment is not resolved after three (3) attempts, Company may cancel the membership immediately. Any amounts previously paid are forfeited and non-refundable. Member remains responsible for any Early Termination Fee under Section 6.
  • Company is not liable for regulatory violations or service disruptions arising from suspension due to Member's failure to maintain a valid payment method.

3.4 Reinstatement. If cancelled for non-payment, Member may reinstate within thirty (30) days by paying all outstanding balances and providing a valid payment method at the same pricing as the cancelled agreement. After thirty (30) days, Member must execute a new Membership Agreement at then-current pricing, and a new Initial Term commences.

3.5 Surcharges. Certain conditions may result in additional charges billed separately from the Membership Fee, including but not limited to: access surcharges (Section 10), after-hours service surcharges (Section 9.3), and confined space entry surcharges. Applicable surcharge rates are published by Company and may be updated from time to time.

Section 4

Price Adjustments

4.1 Annual Price Adjustment Cap. Company may increase the Membership Fee upon renewal of any Annual Term by an amount not to exceed ten percent (10%) of the then-current Membership Fee. Company may elect not to increase the Membership Fee at any given renewal.

4.2 Notice of Price Increase. Company will provide Member with written notice of any price increase no less than forty-five (45) days prior to the Renewal Date on which the new pricing takes effect. The notice will state the current Membership Fee, the new Membership Fee, and the applicable Renewal Date.

4.3 Member's Right to Cancel Upon Price Increase. If Member does not wish to renew at the increased price, Member may cancel by providing written notice any time before the Renewal Date. The thirty (30) day early cancellation notice requirement under Section 6 does not apply. If Company provides less than thirty (30) days' notice of a price increase, Member may cancel with correspondingly less notice, effective as of the Renewal Date, without triggering Early Termination fees.

4.4 Acceptance of New Price. If Member does not cancel before the Renewal Date, continued payment at the new rate constitutes acceptance of the price increase.

Section 5

Cancellation and Non-Renewal

5.1 Non-Renewal. Member may elect not to renew this Agreement at the end of any Annual Term by providing written notice at least thirty (30) days prior to the applicable Renewal Date. Non-renewal does not constitute early termination and no Early Termination Fee applies.

5.2 Cancellation by Company. Company may cancel this Agreement upon thirty (30) days' written notice to Member, or immediately upon: (a) Member's failure to remedy a material breach within ten (10) days of written notice; (b) non-payment as described in Section 3.3; or (c) Member's repeated failure to provide adequate access as described in Section 10.

Section 6

Early Termination by Member

6.1 Early Termination Notice. Member may cancel this Agreement before the end of the then-current Annual Term by providing thirty (30) days' advance written notice to Company. Cancellation takes effect thirty (30) days after Company receives that notice.

6.2 Service During Notice Period. Because Membership Fees are billed in advance, service continues through the end of the notice period. If Member has paid for a service that has not yet been delivered, Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver that service before the cancellation date. Company is not obligated to refund Membership Fees paid for the notice period.

6.3 Early Termination Fee. Upon early termination, Member shall pay Company an Early Termination Fee equal to the positive difference, if any, between: (a) the aggregate Retail Price of all services received by Member since the commencement of the then-current Annual Term; and (b) the aggregate Membership Fees paid by Member since the commencement of the then-current Annual Term. If services received at Retail Price equal or are less than Membership Fees paid, no Early Termination Fee is owed.

How the Early Termination Fee works: The Membership Plan is priced to deliver long-term value — members effectively prepay for high-value services (such as inspections and line jetting) spread across the Annual Term. If a member cancels after receiving services whose Retail Price exceeds what they have paid in Membership Fees, the difference is due. Company will provide a written calculation within five (5) business days of receiving an Early Termination notice.

6.4 Payment of Fee. Early Termination Fees are due and payable on the effective cancellation date.

6.5 No Refunds. Except as provided in Section 4.3, Membership Fees paid are non-refundable.

Section 7

Description of Services

7.1 Scope. Company will perform the grease interceptor maintenance and plumbing services specified in Member's Membership Plan. Services are limited to those described in the Membership Agreement. Services not included in the Membership Plan are available at Retail Prices.

7.2 Standard Service Inclusions. Unless otherwise specified in the Membership Agreement, every Service Visit includes a complete pump-out and physical scrape of the grease interceptor, a baffle and flow control device inspection, a clean-water flow test, a dated service report with technician signature, a waste manifest for regulatory records, and photo documentation of condition before and after service.

7.3 FOG Compliance Documentation. Company will provide service documentation consistent with applicable municipal FOG ordinance requirements for Member's service location. Member is responsible for retaining and submitting that documentation to regulatory authorities as required.

7.4 Biological Treatment. Where the Membership Plan includes biological grease-digesting treatment, that treatment is applied as a supplement to physical cleaning and does not replace scheduled pump-out service. Company makes no guarantee that biological treatment alone will eliminate FOG accumulation or ensure regulatory compliance.

7.5 Condition of Equipment. Services are performed on Member's existing grease interceptor equipment. Company will notify Member of material deficiencies or conditions discovered during a Service Visit. Company is not responsible for pre-existing deficiencies or regulatory non-compliance arising from conditions prior to enrollment.

7.6 Waste Hauling and Disposal. Company is a licensed waste hauler and permitted disposal facility. All waste collected from Member's grease interceptor will be transported and disposed of by Company in compliance with applicable state and local regulations. Member bears no liability for waste disposal once collected by Company during a Service Visit.

7.7 Plan Modifications. Company reserves the right to modify, update, or restructure Membership Plan offerings from time to time. Such changes do not affect the terms of any active Membership Agreement until renewal. At renewal, any plan changes take effect as part of the then-current terms, subject to Member's right to cancel under Sections 4.3 and 5.1.

Member acknowledges and accepts that: (a) Company does not guarantee service on any specific day of the week or at any specific time of day; (b) scheduled service windows may change from visit to visit; and (c) Company will make commercially reasonable efforts to schedule services at times that minimize disruption to Member's food service operations.

9.2 Advance Notice. Company will provide Member with at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance notice of each scheduled Service Visit, delivered to Member's email or phone number on file. Member is responsible for keeping contact information current.

9.3 After-Hours Service. Normal Service Hours are 7:00 AM to 3:00 PM, Monday through Friday, excluding Company-recognized holidays. Service requested or required outside Normal Service Hours is subject to after-hours surcharges as published by Company. Surcharge rates are stated in the Membership Agreement or current rate schedule. Emergency after-hours response is available to Members on a priority basis, subject to technician availability.

Where a Membership Plan includes priority scheduling as a stated benefit, Company will use commercially reasonable efforts to fulfill service requests within the timeframe specified in the Membership Agreement. The credit amount will be specified in the Membership Agreement and is Member's sole remedy for scheduling delays.

9.5 Rescheduling. Company may reschedule a Service Visit with twenty-four (24) hours' notice due to operational, weather, or staffing conditions. Member may request rescheduling with at least twenty-four (24) hours' advance notice. A rescheduled visit counts as a service occurrence for the applicable billing period.

9.6 Service Disruption Acknowledgment. Member acknowledges that grease interceptor maintenance may temporarily generate odors. Company will take reasonable steps to minimize disruption but cannot guarantee an odor-free service process. Member is responsible for informing its staff and patrons as necessary.

Section 10

Access Requirements

10.1 Member's Obligation. Member agrees to provide Company's technicians with safe, free, and unencumbered access to all grease interceptors and associated equipment at each scheduled Service Visit. Access means the grease interceptor lid, access port, and surrounding area are clear of obstructions — including stored inventory, equipment, appliances, refuse containers, or any other materials — so that the interceptor can be serviced without removal or relocation of items by Company's technicians.

10.2 Access Surcharge. If technicians arrive and encounter obstructions that Company elects to clear, Company may charge an access surcharge at the published hourly rate, prorated to the nearest fifteen (15) minutes, for the time spent clearing those obstructions. Company is not obligated to clear obstructions and may cancel the Service Visit under Section 10.3 instead.

Company's technicians are not authorized to perform heavy lifting or move heavy equipment, cooking appliances, or structural components. If access requires such effort, Member must clear the area before the visit. Company may cancel the Service Visit if it cannot be safely performed.

10.3 Right to Cancel Service Visit. Company may cancel a Service Visit without penalty if technicians reasonably determine that access cannot be safely or reasonably obtained. A cancelled visit under this Section counts as a scheduled service occurrence for the billing period, and no credit or refund will be issued.

10.4 Repeated Access Failures. Two (2) or more access failures within any twelve (12) month period may be treated as a material breach and grounds for termination under Section 5.2.

10.5 Safety. Member represents that its premises comply with applicable safety regulations and that no known hazardous conditions exist at the service location. Member shall notify Company of any known or suspected hazardous conditions before a scheduled visit.
