Section 6

Early Termination by Member

6.1 Early Termination Notice. Member may cancel this Agreement prior to the expiration of the then-current Annual Term by providing thirty (30) days' advance written notice to Company (“Early Termination Notice”). Cancellation will become effective thirty (30) days after Company receives the Early Termination Notice (the “Early Termination Date”).

6.2 Service During Notice Period. Because Membership Fees are billed in advance, service shall continue through the Early Termination Date. If Member has not yet received a scheduled Service Visit for a billing period already paid, Company will complete that Service Visit prior to the Early Termination Date. Company is under no obligation to issue any refund for Membership Fees paid for the notice period.

6.3 Early Termination Fee — General Principle. Upon early termination, Member shall pay Company an Early Termination Fee equal to the positive difference, if any, between: (a) the aggregate Retail Price of all services received by Member since the commencement of the then-current Annual Term; and (b) the aggregate Membership Fees paid by Member since the commencement of the then-current Annual Term. If the aggregate Retail Price of services received is equal to or less than the Membership Fees paid, no Early Termination Fee is owed.

6.4 Early Termination Fee — Plan-Specific Calculations. The following rules apply to services received that include high-value bundled components:

6.4(a) Annual Line Jetting (HGI Biweekly/Monthly Care & GGI Professional)

Annual line jetting is a bundled service with a Retail Price of $768.00. If Member has received the annual jetting service prior to the Early Termination Date, the Early Termination Fee for jetting alone shall be calculated as:

Jetting ETF = ( (12 − N) / 12 ) × $768.00

Where N = the number of complete months Member has paid Membership Fees in the then-current Annual Term.

Example: Member paid 7 months and has received jetting. Jetting ETF = (5/12) × $768 = $320.00.

6.4(b) Quarterly Line Jetting (GGI Premier — 4x per year)

GGI Premier includes quarterly jetting (4 visits per year) with a Retail Price of $768.00 per jetting visit. For each jetting visit received in the then-current Annual Term, the Early Termination Fee for that visit shall be calculated as:

Per-Jetting ETF = ( (3 − M) / 3 ) × $768.00

Where M = the number of complete months of the current jetting cycle (3-month period) for which Member has paid.

Example: Member is in Month 2 of their second jetting cycle and has received the second jetting. M=2. ETF = (1/3) × $768 = $256.00.

6.4(c) Camera Inspection

Camera inspection is a bundled service with a Retail Price of $500.00. If Member has received a camera inspection during the then-current Annual Term, the Early Termination Fee for the camera inspection shall be calculated as:

Camera ETF = ( (12 − N) / 12 ) × $500.00

Where N = the number of complete months Member has paid Membership Fees in the then-current Annual Term.

For GGI Premier (biannual cameras), this calculation applies independently to each camera inspection received.

6.4(d) Grease Trap / Interceptor Pump-Out Services

For plans where Member has received one or more pump-out service visits but has not yet paid sufficient Membership Fees to cover the Retail Price of those visits, the Early Termination Fee shall equal the positive difference between the Retail Price of all pump-out visits received and the total Membership Fees attributable to pump-out services paid. Retail Prices for pump-out services are set forth in Schedule B.

6.4(e) Partially Paid Quarterly or Bi-Monthly Service Cycles

For Member on a quarterly (4x/year) or bi-monthly (6x/year) plan, where Member has paid for a portion of a service cycle but has not yet received the scheduled service, Member has the following options:

  1. Option A: Receive the scheduled service early, prior to the Early Termination Date, and pay the Early Termination Fee equal to the positive difference between the Retail Price of that service and the amount of Membership Fees attributable to that cycle paid to date.
  2. Option B: Forfeit the Membership Fees paid for the current incomplete service cycle. No refund will be issued for forfeited amounts under this option.

6.5 Total Early Termination Fee. The total Early Termination Fee is the sum of all applicable per-service calculations under Section 6.4. Company shall provide Member with a written Early Termination Fee calculation within five (5) business days of receiving the Early Termination Notice. Early Termination Fees are due and payable on the Early Termination Date.

6.6 No Refunds. Except as expressly provided in Section 4.3 (cancellation following inadequate notice of price increase), Membership Fees paid are non-refundable.

Description of Services

7.1 Scope of Services. Company agrees to perform the grease interceptor maintenance and plumbing services specified in Member's selected Membership Plan as described in the Membership Plan Selection section and Schedule A of this Agreement. Services are limited to those expressly described. Services not listed in the Membership Plan or Schedule A are available at Retail Prices.

7.2 Service Inclusions — All Tiers. Every Service Visit at every plan tier includes: (a) complete pump-out and physical scrape; (b) baffle and flow control device inspection; (c) clean-water flow test before departure; (d) dated service report with technician signature; (e) waste manifest for regulatory records; and (f) photo documentation of condition before and after service.

Plans that include ZoomBio treatment will receive a biological grease-digesting treatment applied at each Service Visit (and via automatic dosing unit for GGI Premier). ZoomBio is intended to reduce FOG accumulation between service visits and control odors. ZoomBio treatment is used as a supplement to physical cleaning and does not replace the scheduled pump-out service. Company makes no guarantee that ZoomBio will eliminate FOG accumulation or ensure regulatory compliance on its own.

7.5 Additional Services. Services outside the scope of Member's Membership Plan (including but not limited to emergency plumbing, drain line repairs, additional camera inspections beyond those included, and pipe jetting beyond included allotments) are available to Members at the applicable discounted rate specified in the Membership Plan, applied to the then-current Retail Price for such services. 7.6 Condition of Equipment. Services are performed on Member's existing grease interceptor equipment. Company is not responsible for pre-existing deficiencies, equipment failures, or regulatory non-compliance arising from the condition of Member's equipment prior to enrollment.

7.7 Waste Hauling and Disposal. Company is a licensed waste hauler and permitted disposal facility. All waste collected from Member's grease interceptor will be transported and disposed of by Company in compliance with applicable state and local regulations. Member shall have no liability for waste disposal once it has been collected by Company during a Service Visit.

Section 10

Access Requirements

Member is responsible for ensuring that access conditions are maintained at all times and are ready for service at the time of each scheduled Service Visit.

In the event that Company technicians arrive at Member's premises for a scheduled Service Visit and encounter obstructions that require effort to clear before the grease interceptor can be serviced, Company reserves the right to charge an access surcharge at the rate of $275.00 per hour (prorated to the nearest fifteen (15) minute increment) for the time spent by Company's technicians to clear such obstructions (“Access Surcharge”). 10.4 Repeated Access Failures. If Member fails to provide adequate access on two (2) or more occasions within any twelve (12) month period, Company may treat such failure as a material breach of this Agreement and may terminate the Agreement under Section 5.2.
